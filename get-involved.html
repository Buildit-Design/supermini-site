Join now

Whether you already have a car, are looking to buy one, or are just working out if this is for you, the first step is the same. Fill in the form and one of the committee will be in touch to talk you through membership, the car and your first round.

  • Membership runs for the calendar year and covers the championship, the paddock and the support that comes with it.
  • We will point you at the right Motorsport Australia licence for where you are up to.
  • Ready-to-race cars come up regularly, and we can tell you what to look for before you buy.

Prefer email? Write to admin@supermini.au.

Before your first round

What else you need

A competition licence

You will need a Motorsport Australia competition licence. Tell us where you are up to and we will point you at the right level.

A car

An R53 Cooper S built to Cooper Class spec, or a more developed Mini for Unlimited. Buy one ready to race or build your own.

An entry

Enter the round through the organising club. On REC membership we do that part for you.

The car

Get a SuperMini

The Cooper Class car is a BMW R53 Cooper S built to the series spec, while the Unlimited Class welcomes faster, more developed Minis. There are two ways to get one:

  • Buy one — ready-to-race SuperMinis come up for sale on my105, Australia's race car marketplace, and we list them here when members are selling.
  • Build your own — start with an eligible R53 Cooper S and build it to the published specifications.
