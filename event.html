The circuit

Queensland Raceway

The SuperMini Challenge heads to Queensland for Round 4 of the Rubber Craft Queensland State Championship at Queensland Raceway, Ipswich — a fast, flat circuit just west of Brisbane.

Both the Cooper and Unlimited classes will be on track across the weekend. Check back here for the full timetable, entry details and the latest event information.
