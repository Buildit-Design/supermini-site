For sale: a race-ready Cooper Class car

Cooper Class car for sale

A Cooper Class car has come up for sale. It is race ready as it sits and eligible for state and national rounds.

The car was built by STZ Automotive and raced through the 2026 championship. The engine has been rebuilt and is two races old.

What comes with it

  • 2005 Mini Cooper S, built as a Cooper Class car
  • Current Motorsport Australia logbook
  • Freshly rebuilt engine, two races old
  • MoTeC C125 dash and data logger
  • Full roll cage
  • Spare set of rims with new tyres

The car is in Clontarf, Queensland. Price, photos and contact details are on the my105 listing.

If you are looking at getting into the class, a logbooked car with a fresh engine is a much easier start than building one. Give us a call if you want to check what the rules mean for a car before you buy it.
