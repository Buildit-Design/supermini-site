For sale: two Cooper Class cars plus spares

Cars 27 and 28 up for sale as a package or separately

Two more Cooper Class cars have come onto the market, and this time there is a choice. The #27 and #28 SuperMinis are for sale, together with two spare Cooper S cars, from Paul Brelsford.

Both race cars were prepared by STZ Automotive and are described as turn-key. Car 27 has a fully built race engine and a championship-winning history in the class. Car 28 runs a standard engine, which makes it a lower-cost way in for someone starting out. The two spare Cooper S cars are offered as donors or parts cars.

What is on offer

  • Car 27 — 2005 Mini Cooper S, fully built race engine, championship history, $50,000
  • Car 28 — 2005 Mini Cooper S, standard engine, $35,000
  • Two spare Cooper S cars, $4,000 each
  • Both race cars STZ-prepared, caged and logbooked
  • Package deals considered

The cars are in The Gap, Brisbane, and the seller has offered to hand over at Warwick if that suits. The seller notes they are being sold well below build and development cost. Photos, pricing and contact details are on the my105 listing.

If you are weighing up a build against a bought car, this is the kind of listing worth a look: a developed, logbooked car that has been raced in the class is a far quicker start than building one, and we are happy to talk through what the current rules mean for either car before you commit.
